Ready, Set, Pickle!

Get Game Ready

You don't need to be an expert or have expensive equipment to get started. A few essentials, a little preparation, and a willingness to learn are all you need for your first game.

What Do You Need to Play?

Starting pickleball is easier than you think. Here's what to bring to your first game.

A Pickleball Paddle

Choose a comfortable paddle that feels easy to control. Beginners can start with an affordable paddle or borrow one while learning.

Pickleballs

Pickleballs are lightweight plastic balls with holes. Indoor and outdoor balls have different designs, so choose the type that matches your playing location.

Comfortable Court Shoes

Wear athletic shoes designed for court movement. Good grip and side-to-side support help you move more comfortably.

Water and Comfortable Clothing

Bring water, wear clothes that allow you to move freely, and consider sun protection when playing outdoors.

Before Your First Game

Feeling a little nervous? That's normal! These simple steps can help you feel more comfortable on the court.

Your First-Game Game Plan

  1. Find a local court that welcomes beginners or offers open play.
  2. Review the serving and two-bounce rules.
  3. Arrive early enough to warm up and practice hitting the ball.
  4. Let other players know you're still learning.
  5. Focus on having fun instead of winning every rally.

Looking for somewhere to play? Visit the USA Pickleball website for resources to help you find opportunities to play.
