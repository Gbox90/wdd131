Everyone Starts Somewhere

Pickleball is a paddle sport that combines elements of tennis, badminton, and ping-pong. It's played on a smaller court and is enjoyed by people of different ages and skill levels.

Whether you're picking up a paddle for the first time or joining friends for a casual game, Just A Little Pickle is here to make learning simple, approachable, and fun.
