Just A Little Pickle

Beginner's guide to pickleball - Website Plan

Site Name

Proposed domain: justalittlepickle.com

Just A Little Pickle helps people who are new to pickleball learn the fundamentals of the game in a simple, approachable way.

Site Purpose

The website will provide beginner-friendly explanations of the rules, scoring, court layout, basic equipment, and tips for preparing for a first game.

Scenarios

  • What are the rules?
  • How can I learn pickleball scoring without getting confused?
  • What kind of paddle should I get?
  • Where do I play?

Color Scheme

The website will use a light and fresh color palette, with shades of green and white to reflect the outdoor and sporty nature of pickleball. It also uses a warm, retro Florida-inspired palette to create a welcoming atmosphere.

  • Palm Green (#264D36): headings and borders
  • Orange (#E9783D): buttons and accents
  • Cream (#FFF4DC): page backgrounds
  • Ink (#292D25): paragraph text

Typography

The website will use the DM Sans font for a clean and modern look, ensuring readability and a friendly appearance. Bold weights will be used for headings, while regular weights will be used for paragraphs and general content.

Example body text:
"Welcome to Just A Little Pickle! Here you'll find everything you need to get started with pickleball."
